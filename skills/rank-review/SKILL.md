---
name: rank-review
description: Find organic growth priorities and explain rank drops using Stora search visibility, dated rankings and listing evidence for one app, store and market.
---

Use `get_context` and select the app, platform, storefront and dates. Read `get_health`, `get_runs`, `get_digest` and the relevant pages of `get_keywords_table`. For material changes, inspect `get_keyword_history` and `get_listing`. Distinguish a first observation or coverage change from a movement between independently captured days.

Report the few changes that matter with the keyword, market, previous and latest observed ranks, observation dates and source freshness. Explain incomplete scans or capture depth before interpreting missing ranks. Do not call a missing rank a store indexing penalty, and do not interpret a new baseline as a new competitor entering.

Use relevance to the app's current product and live localized listing when selecting priorities. Brand terms, direct rivals, adjacent products and generic searches have different intent. Rank gains alone do not prove downloads, purchases, revenue or causation. A listing edit and a rank move can be correlated without demonstrating the edit caused the move.

Finish with a small set of evidence-supported next steps and what observation would validate each. Change tracking or research decisions only when requested. A scan can be queued with `trigger_scan` when authorized; distinguish queued work from completed evidence.
